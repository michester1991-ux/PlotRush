The Billionaire's Secret Son

Michael was only eight years old when tragedy changed his life forever. After losing his parents in a terrible accident, he was separated from his wealthy family and raised in a struggling orphanage.

Unknown to him, his grandfather was one of the richest businessmen in the city. For years, the old man searched endlessly for his missing grandson but never found him.

Michael grew up suffering through poverty, rejection, and heartbreak. Yet despite his difficult life, he remained kind and hardworking.

Everything changed the day he saved a powerful CEO during a dangerous robbery. That single moment uncovered shocking secrets about his real identity.

Suddenly, Michael found himself pulled into a world of wealth, betrayal, family conflict, and hidden enemies determined to destroy him.
